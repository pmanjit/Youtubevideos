# BRIEF — cold_snap

## Working title
**THE COLD IS THE CLOCK**

## Logline
The world's temperature drops and does not come back. You survive it — but the cold is not what kills you. Everyone around you is out of food, out of heat, and running the same math. This is a second-person survival briefing on the real threat after the drop.

## Format
- Platform: YouTube (16:9)
- Target runtime: 5 minutes
- Target word count: ~930 words (930 = 5 × 60 × 3.1 wps)
- POV: **Second-person ("you")** — the viewer is the survivor
- Genre: explainer / survival doc, hybrid — instructional but tense, no named cast
- Captions: none burned in

## Thesis spine
1. The cold is the visible threat and the invisible timer.
2. Heat, water, and calories are the actual resources — and they are finite.
3. Scarcity does not make people evil; it makes them *calculating*.
4. The first 72 hours are won on preparation; after that, on **trust you vet**.
5. Team up — but only on terms you set before you need them.

## Channel positioning
A NEW channel, deliberately distinct from **inkexplainer**.
- No chalk-on-paper, no sketch-notebook, no hand-drawn diagram voiceover tropes.
- Visual language **is** the user-supplied stick-figure reference, reproduced in code (`tools/stick_snap.py`).
- Tone: cold, procedural, second-person. Calm instructions over dread. Never cheerful.
